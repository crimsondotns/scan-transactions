Transfers Table Variants

Four layouts for the transfers table with a Price column, each rendered from the 20 sample transfers (hood, base, arb, op, monad). Price is the per-token price at the time of the transfer as sent by the feed; Approve rows show the token's current price instead. Flagged tokens carry a text badge and count as $0.

1Classic Dense

The current column order, tightened: 48px rows, 13px tabular numerals right-aligned, hairline under rows only. Price sits between Token and Amount so a swap reads as two aligned lines: token, its price, its amount.

Best for desk users who already know the table and want more rows per screen without learning a new layout.

Nine columns. 24h change omitted: it does not fit at this density.

2Token-First Ledger

Token leads the row, then one numeric block reads left to right as amount × price = value. Counterparty and tx hash drop to a muted line under the type pill, so the eye lands on what moved and what it was worth.

Best for reviewing value: “was that swap at a fair price” or “how much did this airdrop claim to be worth”.

Seven columns. 24h change shown next to the price.

3Two-Row Expandable

A compact primary row (time, type, token, amount, value) and a chevron. Expanding a row reveals a detail grid with price, 24h change, from and to, gas, tx hash and chain. The same markup collapses to a phone list.

Best for phones and fast scanning of long histories where price and counterparty matter only for a few rows.

Five columns visible. Expansion is a button with aria-expanded; the first row starts open.

4Card Grid

Each transfer is a card: a header with type, time and chain, a body with one line per token leg (logo, symbol, amount, price, value), and a footer with counterparty, gas and tx hash. Two columns on desktop, one on phones.

Best for wallets with a few transfers, or a phone-first product. Density is the trade-off: about 6 transfers per desktop screen versus 15 or more in the tables.

Everything visible, nothing sortable. 24h change shown next to the symbol.
